algebra: equations, polynomials, identities, algebraic functions, linear or abstract algebra, and symbolic algebraic structure whose exact relations do the central work
geometry: Euclidean or non-Euclidean figures, incidence, transformations, metric, distance, angle, area, and spatial configurations whose geometric structure does the central work
number_theory: integers with indispensable prime, divisibility, congruence, Diophantine, valuation, gcd/lcm, or arithmetic-function structure; an integer input or answer alone does not qualify
discrete_mathematics: combinatorics, graph theory, finite configurations, arrangements, selections, discrete processes, and enumerative structures whose finite-discrete organization does the central work
applied_mathematics: probability, statistics, numerical mathematics, modeling, mathematical physics, finance, information theory, or another application-driven mathematical formulation; a story wrapper alone does not qualify
calculus: limits, continuity, derivatives, integrals, differential equations, infinite series, or continuous analysis for which calculus is indispensable
precalculus: elementary functions, trigonometry, logarithms, exponentials, conics or function graphs, and elementary sequences or function behavior not requiring calculus
